# ordi v1.25.0

The polish pass after the HR release: one dashboard for everyone with the
team calendar on it, personal contacts move out of hardcoded card rows into
custom fields, icons for fields and groups, and a date picker that can reach
a birth year without paging through two decades.

## One dashboard for everyone

- The home dashboard is now identical for every role: my tasks / overdue /
  projects tiles, plus a **Team, next two weeks** card – absences with their
  leave type and end date (pending ones marked), holidays and birthdays,
  grouped by day with Today/Tomorrow labels and a deep link into the HR
  calendar tab (`/people?tab=calendar`).
- The finance and pipeline widgets (receivables, deals by stage, sales queue
  tiles) leave the home screen – they were role-gated, so most people stared
  at gaps where someone else's numbers would be. Money views live in Finance
  and in custom dashboards.
- The activity feed shows actor avatars; the onboarding checklist is
  admin-only and actually disappears once every step is done.

## Personal contacts become custom fields

- The employee card no longer hardcodes personal email, phone or telegram –
  the card keeps what is structural (work email = the login account,
  birthday, documents), and personal contacts are whatever custom fields the
  workspace defines, grouped and access-controlled like everything else.
- The `telegram` column shipped in v1.24.0 is dropped by migration; personal
  email/phone columns remain for the directory's account matching but are no
  longer shown or edited on the card.

## Icons for fields and groups

- Custom fields and field groups can carry an **icon** from a curated lucide
  set, picked in their edit dialogs and rendered wherever they show: record
  cards, the profile questionnaire, the settings lists and the RBAC access
  matrix.

## Fixes

- **Date picker**: the title now opens a 12-year grid (paged by 12), a year
  opens the months, a month lands back on the days – picking a birthday no
  longer means scrolling month by month from today to 2007. Typed dates
  (21.08.2007, 2007-08-21) keep working.
- **Comment avatars** show the author's profile photo instead of initials.
- **The people directory** names rows from the HR card (first + last name)
  when a profile exists – an account called just "Vasyl" used to hide the
  surname his card carried.

## Migrations

`0030` field/group icons + drop of the unused employees.telegram column.
