# ordi v1.5.4

Fixes the macOS title bar introduced in v1.5.3: the traffic lights sat on top
of the workspace switcher, and the window could not be dragged at all.

## What was wrong

**The window would not move.** `data-tauri-drag-region` calls the window's
`start_dragging` command, and that command is not part of `core:default` -
it needs `core:window:allow-start-dragging` in the capability file. Without
it every drag was silently denied. With a hidden native title bar and no
working drag region, the window was stuck where it opened.

**The inset went to the wrong column.** The traffic lights float over the
window's top-left corner, which is the sidebar - not the tab strip on the
right. The 84px of clearance was reserved in the strip, where nothing
overlaps, while the sidebar kept its workspace switcher exactly under the
buttons.

## What it looks like now

The sidebar opens with a short empty strip that belongs to the traffic
lights; the workspace switcher starts below them. Tabs, the navigation
arrows and the window buttons share one row, and every gap in both the strip
and that sidebar band drags the window.

## Upgrading

No schema changes, nothing server-side. macOS only; Windows and Linux keep
their native title bars and were never affected.
