# ordi v1.36.0

Invoices can be edited, a due date is optional, dates read as dates, and
the tax row appears only when there is tax.

## Editing an invoice

The invoice page could change notes and terms and nothing else; the create
form asked for a due date only. Now one form creates and edits an invoice
or quote: issue date, due date (or valid-until), currency, document
language, the tax rate applied to every line, a discount and the line
items. **Edit** on the invoice page opens it. Items lock once the invoice
has been sent (cancel and duplicate to change them, as before); dates,
currency, language, tax and discount stay editable at any status.

## A due date is optional

The form used to set today's date when none was picked, so every invoice
carried a deadline. `invoices.due_date` is nullable now (migration 0040):
an undated invoice is open but never overdue, gets no payment reminders,
and sits outside the overdue list and the expected-payments calendar. The
invoice page says "No due date"; the PDF and the public page print no row.

## Dates and rows on the document

- Dates read as dates in the document's language: "28 September 2026" or
  "28 вересня 2026 р.", not `2026-09-28`.
- The internal status (Draft, Sent, Viewed) no longer prints on the PDF.
- The tax row appears only when there is tax, named after the rate when
  every taxed line shares one: "Tax (VAT 20%)". A discount row appears when
  a discount applies. Both on the PDF, the public page and the invoice page.

## Upgrading

`docker compose pull && up -d` (Dokploy: Redeploy). One migration runs on
boot and makes `invoices.due_date` nullable; existing invoices keep their
dates. Tax rates live in Settings → Finance as before; pick one on an
invoice from the Edit dialog or the create form.

The desktop app is unchanged apart from its version number.
