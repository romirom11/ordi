# ordi v1.0.0

The first release of **ordi** – a self-hosted operations platform for agencies and product teams: projects and tasks, CRM, knowledge base, time tracking, finance and people – in one fast, keyboard-friendly app.

## Highlights

### Projects & tasks
- Linear-style task pages, board/list/spreadsheet views, cycles, filters and display options
- Quick-create composer (`C`) with status, priority, assignees, labels, due date and "create more"
- Project overview: summary, resources, milestones, updates and a live progress chart
- Configurable project types (client work, internal, product – or your own) that drive behavior like client binding and revenue attribution
- Per-project access control for private work

### Editor & collaboration
- Notion-style rich text editor everywhere (slash menu, markdown shortcuts)
- `@` people mentions and `#` references to tasks, KB pages, companies and invoices
- Realtime updates over SSE: assignments and mentions arrive instantly with a toast and a sound – no refresh needed
- In-app tabs (Ctrl/⌘-click), global search, notifications inbox

### CRM
- Companies and deals merged into one flow with a pipeline board
- Client portal links, intake forms and IMAP email intake

### Knowledge base
- Spaces with access control, nested pages, publishing, right-click context menus

### Finance
- Invoices and quotes with branded, localized emails and public payment pages
- Double-entry ledger core: accounts, balanced postings, automatic entries from invoices, payments and expenses
- Expenses including recurring ones (subscriptions), project profitability, manual income

### People & time
- Employee cards, org structure (departments and positions), compensation history
- Time tracking with timers and reports, resourcing planner, dashboards

### Platform
- First-run setup wizard, module toggles (turn off what you don't use)
- Roles and granular permissions, uk/en localization, dark/light theme
- Slack integration (connect once, pick a channel per project), GitHub integration
- Built-in MCP server so AI assistants can work with your workspace
- Desktop app (Tauri) for macOS, Windows and Linux – artifacts attached to this release
- Docker-based deployment (`docs/deployment.md`)
