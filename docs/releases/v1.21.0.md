# ordi v1.21.0

The lead card's most informative fields were its least readable. Product,
signal and source lived in the 320px properties rail: values cut to one
ellipsised line, labels wrapping onto two or three, and the only way to read
what a field actually said was to click into it and start editing. This
release moves prose out of the rail on the CRM detail pages and leaves the
rail doing what a rail is good at – short relational facts.

## Lead details in the wide column

- A new **Details** card sits between the next-action card and Qualification:
  product / service, signal, source, source link, suggested channel and score
  in a two-column grid, labels above values. Product and signal are
  multi-line – long values wrap and read fully instead of ellipsising.
- The source link renders as its hostname (`gaiaeducation.org`, not a full
  URL nobody can parse at a glance). The full URL is still what you edit, and
  the header's Open source button still opens it. The source-checked date
  became a footnote under the link instead of a rail row of its own.
- The rail keeps status, company, contact, owner, created and files – every
  label fits on one line again.

## Deal custom fields follow

- Workspace-defined custom fields on a deal render as a **Custom fields**
  card in the main column, in the same two-column grid, instead of a rail
  block with 150px labels. Free-text answers and URLs – what custom fields
  mostly carry – wrap instead of truncating.
- Every field type behaves as before (text, number, url, date, select,
  multiselect, checkbox, user); a read-only URL value additionally wraps
  instead of overflowing the card.
- The deal rail keeps stage, client, project, owner, amount, currency, close
  date, created and files.

Both pages share the new `DetailField` building block, so future detail pages
get the same treatment for free.

## Verified

Typecheck, web tests and the query-shape check pass; both pages were driven
with headless Chromium against a stub API in dark theme – a long Ukrainian
signal value, a long source URL and text/select/url custom fields all render
wrapped and fully readable, with a clean browser console.
