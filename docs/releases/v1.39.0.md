# ordi v1.39.0

A second look for the whole app, **Glass**, and a round of access and finance fixes: API tokens can no longer widen themselves or write when read-only, credit notes count toward every balance, and month-end schedules stay in their month.

## Glass style

Workspace menu → **Style** → **Glass**. It works with both the light and dark theme, and **Classic** (the look so far) stays the default.

Glass draws the same screens as frosted panes over a soft, out-of-focus landscape:

- warm graphite neutrals, with a lime accent on primary actions;
- pill-shaped buttons and switchers, rounder cards, and the Urbanist typeface;
- hovers and selected rows are a light tint over the glass, and a card you point at brightens;
- switches and checkboxes are a calm moss green when on, and the focus ring is a soft sage halo.

The choice is stored per browser, like the theme. Browsers without backdrop blur get solid panes instead of see-through ones.

## Fixes

- **Pipeline cards no longer touch.** In CRM → Pipeline the deal cards in a stage sat edge to edge. They have their usual gap again.
- **Credit notes count toward the balance** on the public invoice page, the invoice PDF (new "Credited" row), the invoice page and company receivables. A fully credited invoice used to still ask the client for the whole total, and "Record payment" was prefilled with an amount the server then refused.
- **Month-end schedules stay in their month.** A monthly, quarterly or yearly schedule due on the 31st rolled into the next month (Jan 31 → Mar 3), so February had no run. Recurring invoices, payments and tasks now land on the last day of a shorter month. A schedule anchored on the 29th–31st stays on the 28th after February.

## Security

- **API tokens cannot mint broader tokens.** Creating a token checked the new scopes against the owner's role, not the token making the call. Token management, two-factor setup and the desktop and OAuth approvals now need a signed-in session. API tokens can no longer list, create or revoke tokens.
- **Two-factor authentication can only be turned off with a code.** Setting it up again used to switch an enabled second factor off without one.
- **Read-only tokens no longer write.** Lead conversion and expense categories went through for read-only tokens. Any mutating request with a read-only token is now refused, and reads work as before.
- **Cost rates stay with finance.** Time entries carried each person's cost rate, derived from compensation, to anyone who could read all time, including Managers. Cost rates now need the "read costs" finance permission.
- **OAuth redirect URIs** with `javascript:`, `data:`, `vbscript:`, `file:`, `blob:` or `about:` are refused at registration and again at approval. The check at approval also covers clients registered before this release.
- **MCP `upload_attachment` fetches public URLs only.** Its URL mode fetched any address from inside the API, including loopback, private ranges and cloud metadata, and buffered bodies of any size. Non-public addresses are now refused, every redirect hop is checked again, the body is cut off at the upload limit, and a fetch times out after 20 s.

## Also

- Better keyboard and overlay behaviour, recovery of unsaved drafts in editors, responsive layouts, finance form validation, and clearer errors when a page fails to load.
- Task and knowledge base edits now update open tabs in real time.
- The macOS desktop app has workspace actions in its menu bar.

No migrations.
