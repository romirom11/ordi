# ordi v1.36.1

The totals block on an invoice no longer repeats one number three times.

## Only rows that add information

An untaxed, unpaid invoice printed Subtotal, Total and Balance due, all
187.00. Every row now has to say something the previous one did not:

- the **subtotal** appears only next to a discount or a tax row;
- the **total** appears only next to a payment ("Amount paid");
- otherwise the block is a single **Balance due** row, which reads
  **Paid in full** once the invoice is settled.

That last row is no longer printed on a filled colour block: it is a bold
row over an accent-coloured rule, with the amount in the accent colour, on
the PDF and on the public invoice page alike.

Quotes keep one Total row, preceded by the subtotal only when a discount
or tax explains the difference. The same rule applies on the PDF, the
public invoice and quote pages, and the invoice page in the app.

## Upgrading

`docker compose pull && up -d` (Dokploy: Redeploy). No migrations. The
desktop app is unchanged apart from its version number.
