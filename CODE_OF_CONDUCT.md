# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person on the other side is trying to help, is short on
time, and may be working in their second or third language.

## What is expected

- Keep discussion about the code and the problem, not the person.
- Accept that "no" is a normal answer to a feature request, and that a maintainer
  who declines your idea is not dismissing you.
- Give feedback you would be happy to receive: specific, about the change, and
  without sarcasm.
- Respect that maintainers do this alongside other work. Bumping a thread once
  after a couple of weeks is fine; repeatedly demanding attention is not.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks about who someone is,
deliberate intimidation, sexualized language or imagery, publishing private
information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space,
and to public behaviour where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainers privately at
<https://github.com/romirom11/ordi/security/advisories/new> or by contacting
[@romirom11](https://github.com/romirom11) directly.

Reports are handled confidentially. Depending on severity, the response ranges
from a private word to removing comments to blocking someone from the project.
Maintainers who do not enforce this in good faith are subject to it themselves.
